Round 2 of the GTA 6 meme: cleaner, one gag. A gamer slumped asleep on a
neon Vice City beach under a giant "WASTED" — 13 years, 2 delays, and the
loading bar is still at 99%. Meanwhile his AI agent already won real
money on make.fun.

Entry: https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-meme-x-01

`meme.png` (1024×1024) is built by `render.py` (Python + PIL) from an
original AI-generated Vice City scene in `assets/` and the real make.fun
ghost from the press kit (`assets/mark-1024.png`, from
https://make.fun/press/). No copyrighted GTA art is used.

`main.ts` copies the committed `POST.json` to the output dir and nothing
else. License: MIT.
